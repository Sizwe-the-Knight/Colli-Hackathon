# Devpost — judge testing notes

**Project:** Colli — make sense of your AI

**Intended track:** Best Apps and Agents

## Try the demo in a few minutes

1. Open the live URL. Render Free may need about a minute to wake after inactivity;
   wait for the sign-in page before trying again. Free-tier availability is not guaranteed.
2. Choose **Request access** to register. Use your own name/email and a unique test
   password of at least 15 characters (the backend also limits passwords to 72 UTF-8
   bytes). Do not reuse an important password. No provider keys or payment are needed.
3. Choose provider preferences during onboarding and save. This does **not** connect
   external accounts or import your activity.
4. Read the Daily Executive Brief: what happened, why it matters and what to do next.
   The short opening reveal is cosmetic; reduced-motion preferences skip it.
   **Demo workspace** identifies invented records, not connected account activity.
   The **Illustrative 86/100** uses a separate 30-day example; your account remains
   **Not assessed**. Select **How this score is calculated** for its four evidence
   ratios. Expanding these details makes no AI call.
   Select **Explain with NVIDIA Nemotron** once to request a genuine
   explanation through Nebius, using only that synthetic example. Success is
   explicitly attributed; unavailable generation keeps the deterministic wording.
   Refreshing reuses an accepted result without another paid call. No API key is
   needed from judges. If public capacity is exhausted, contact the founder for
   designation of your existing account to the reserved judge allowance.
5. Select **Continue to Dashboard**, then explore the sidebar. Operational figures
   are labelled samples. Settings contains a planned, non-interactive Colli Village
   preview beneath Language & Region.
6. Refresh to see the signed-in experience persist. Use the profile menu to log out
   when finished. Privacy and Terms are public; account/privacy requests go to
   **a.a.downsouth@gmail.com**. There is no automated deletion control.

with typed evidence-approved output, deterministic fallback and bounded spending.
